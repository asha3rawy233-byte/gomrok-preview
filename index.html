Two zones, one site

Bonded or non-bonded?

One question decides it: have customs duties been paid on your goods yet?

NOT CLEARED YET

Bonded zone

For imports that have not passed customs.

  • Goods stay under customs supervision until you release them
  • Duties are paid at release, not on arrival
  • Closed warehouses and open yards
  • Reached from the port by Trip Ticket, cleared on site
  • [≈50,400 m² · 30+ containers a day — to confirm]

Best for · Importers who want their goods near Cairo before clearance

CLEARED OR LOCAL

Non-bonded zone

For local goods and imports that are already cleared.

  • No customs procedures on entry or exit
  • Goods move in and out whenever you need them
  • Closed warehouses and open yards
  • Chilled and frozen rooms
  • [≈103,600 m² closed · ≈100,000 m² open yards — to confirm]

Best for · Distribution stock, FMCG, food and raw materials

Trip Ticket

From the port to our gate. Then customs.

A Trip Ticket moves your shipment from the port to GOMROK’s bonded zone under customs seal. Clearance happens here in 6th of October, not at the port.
